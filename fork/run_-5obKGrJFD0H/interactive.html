The finding

The question. When a new scientific concept first shows up in a subfield outside its home, most such entries fade. Which ones take root?

The answer. Look at the words the concept arrives with. The study measures, for each , the of its partner terms, and asks whether it predicts over the next five years.

Arriving as an imported package does not help: of origin companions is null in every fold. The Screen and Held-out folds give concordant estimates, but both were used in pipeline development, so the independent evidence is the MeSH replication.

      What the paper does not show

      • No true held-out test on the main arm. The Held-out fold was used during pipeline development, so its estimates are development-set estimates. The independent evidence is the MeSH replication.
      • The strict specification is underpowered. With concept-by-year and host-by-year fixed effects, the Held-out estimate keeps 250 of 972 events in 30 clusters and is inconclusive (IRR 0.98, 95% CI 0.70 to 1.37).
      • Population bias. The pool is dominated by physics, physical-sciences and computer-science concepts from arXiv, and MeSH covers only biomedicine-to-biomedicine entries. Social sciences, humanities and engineering are untested.
      • Single-paper entries. 87% of co-primary Screen events involve a single entry-year paper. The pooled multi-team effect is positive (IRR 1.28, 95% CI 1.11 to 1.48), but the Held-out fold alone is underpowered for this test.
      • Graded, not a threshold. Binary establishment (at least five newcomer papers in at least three of five years) is null on the Held-out fold (p = 0.16).
      • Cluster-robust p-values over-reject. The estimator rejects 10% to 11% of null shuffles in simulation (target 5%). Wild-cluster and permutation tests agree (Held-out wild p = 0.012, permutation p = 0.026).
      • Host-specificity is weakly tested. The Balassa-index lift has a within-fixed-effect correlation of 0.997 with the log of the continuous share, so it confirms the share rather than adding independent evidence.
      • Adopter evidence is Screen-fold only. The enrichment (OR 3.09) is not independently confirmed and cannot separate absorptive capacity from topical proximity.
