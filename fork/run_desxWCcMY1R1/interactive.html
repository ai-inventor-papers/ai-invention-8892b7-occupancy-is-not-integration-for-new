The finding

What decides whether a new idea takes root in a foreign field?

The study follows 426 emerging concepts and 462,812 OpenAlex works. Each time a concept first shows up in a subfield outside its origin, the authors record its and ask how much of that vocabulary already belongs to the host. That fraction is the . The outcome is over the next five years.

    What the paper does not show

    • The main-arm folds are development sets. Event counts, population sizes and gating decisions were computed on both Screen and Held-out before grafting outcomes were read. The independent evidence is the MeSH replication.
    • The population is narrow. The pool is dominated by physics, physical-sciences and computer-science concepts from arXiv, and MeSH covers biomedicine-to-biomedicine entries only. Social sciences, humanities and engineering are untested.
    • The strictest model is underpowered. With concept-by-year and host-by-year fixed effects the Held-out fold keeps 250 of 972 events in 30 clusters and is inconclusive.
    • Most entries are a single paper. 87% of co-primary Screen events involve one entry-year paper. The pooled multi-team effect is positive (IRR 1.28, 95% CI 1.11 to 1.48), but the Held-out fold alone is underpowered for this test.
    • Cluster-robust p-values run hot. The estimator rejects 10% to 11% of null shuffles in simulation against a 5% target; the wild-cluster bootstrap (Held-out p = 0.012) and permutation tests (p = 0.026) are the safer reading.
    • Host-specificity rests on a weak leg. The Balassa-index lift has a within-fixed-effect correlation of 0.997 with the log of the continuous share, so it confirms the share rather than adding independent evidence.
    • The adopter result is Screen-only. Adopters are enriched for prior exposure to entry partners (OR 3.09; prevalence 0.79 vs. 0.61), but the design cannot separate absorptive capacity from topical proximity.
    • The Held-out closure test borrows controls. 17 of 25 unique controls come from the Screen fold's never-treated pool, and the MeSH closure result is null under the primary label.
