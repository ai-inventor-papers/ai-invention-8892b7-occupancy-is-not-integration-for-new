The finding

The question. When a scientific concept first appears in a new subfield, what decides whether it takes root there?

The paper studies every : the first year a concept shows up outside its origin subfield with at least five co-occurring partner terms. For each event it measures the of those partners, and asks whether it predicts over the next five years.

      What the paper says it does not show

      • What the held-out fold protects. Its outcomes were sealed and opened once, but its entry events, pre-entry features and population size were computed on the same pipeline beforehand, so it guards against outcome-driven choices rather than every choice of frame.
      • Population bias. The pool is dominated by physics, physical-sciences and computer-science concepts from arXiv. MeSH covers only biomedicine-to-biomedicine entries. Social sciences, humanities and engineering are untested.
      • The strict specification is underpowered. With concept-by-year and host-by-year fixed effects, the Held-out estimate is inconclusive (IRR 0.98, 30 clusters).
      • Single-paper entries. 87% of co-primary Screen events involve a single entry-year paper. The pooled multi-team effect is positive (IRR 1.28, 95% CI 1.11 to 1.48), but the Held-out fold alone is underpowered for this test.
      • Graded, not a threshold. is null on the Held-out fold (p = 0.165).
      • Cluster-robust inference over-rejects. It rejects 10% to 11% of null shuffles in simulation (target 5%). Wild-cluster bootstrap and permutation tests agree (Held-out wild p = 0.012, permutation p = 0.026).
      • Host-specificity is weakly tested. The Balassa-index lift measure has a within-fixed-effect correlation of 0.997 with the log of the continuous share, so it confirms the share rather than adding independent evidence.
      • Adopter evidence is Screen-fold only and cannot separate absorptive capacity from topical proximity.
      • RQ1 is R1_DEAD. Under the frozen kill rule, structural precursors of sustained uptake are volume/churn correlates. The one confirmed row, persistent-neighbour closure, had a screen event-study interval that included zero, and 17 of 25 unique Held-out controls come from the Screen fold. The MeSH closure result is directional under the sensitivity label but null under the primary label.
